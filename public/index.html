What you can do

Built for BiTS Software Engineering students.

PSP logs

A Job Number Log and Daily Time Logs: estimate, time and total every programming job, with To Date figures.

GPA tracker

Add courses, grades and credits on the BiTS scale and watch your progress to graduation.

Circular habit tracker

One ring per habit, one segment per day over 90 days. Tap a segment to check it off.

Analytics

GPA by semester, grade distribution, habit streaks and PSP time from your own records.
